The Next Step Ministry House

Helping young adults from the Hill Tribes of Northern Thailand in a safe and inspiring Christian environment.

Life at The Next Step

The Next Step is a ministry house in Chiang Mai where young Thai men from the Hill Tribes live, study, and build a future together. Here are a few of their stories.

Ministry House Overview

The Next Step house is a home base in Chiang Mai where young Thai men from the Hill Tribes live, study, and grow together in a safe, supportive environment.

Our new student, Kaow!

We welcome Kaow (Wanchai), 20, from the Hmong tribe, who just graduated from Payap Technology College where he studied computers.

Gorn finished year one at Payap!

Gorn, 18, of the Lahu tribe, recently finished his first year in the Mechanics program at the technical-school branch of Payap University.

Petro registered for Tech College!

Petro is continuing his education and taking his next step toward a trade and independence.

Visitors, Fellowship, and Activities

Regular visitors join the house for BBQ cookouts, soccer-watching, Bible study, and fellowship, keeping it a true home rather than just a residence.

Sorn is doing freelance video

Sorn is putting his growing video skills to use freelancing, while staying connected to the ministry house community.

Wit is studying in China!

Wit (Surasak) is continuing his education far from home, with the ministry house cheering him on.
