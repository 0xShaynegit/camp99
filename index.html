Camp 99

Camp 99 is a day-use meeting, retreat, and recreation center for Christians.

/ 01

about

Purpose of Camp 99

Camp 99, named after our street address,
is serving the ’99’, the Flock of the Lord!

Camp 99 is a place where Christian people, both Thai and foreigner, can hold meetings, prayer and worship times, come fishing, play games, have picnics and bbq’s, and enjoy fellowship in Jesus’ name!

/ 02

services

Serving the 99

parking

Front parking is available as you enter the property. We’re 8km from the Super Highway, and the location map below shows the way.

sala/cabana

Our beautiful pond has a sala/cabana for up to 8-12 people, with a water cooler and a 300 watt, 4-channel sound system. Meetings, Bible studies, prayer and worship happen here, and so does the eating: picnics, BBQs and shared meals are a big part of Camp 99. A 3mx3m tent/awning is available if needed, and a campfire pit sits near the pond. The full kitchen is available to use through the sliding door, however we are unable to provide meals at this time.

fishing

There are indeed Talapia fish in the pond which we stock every couple of months. We have three spinning rods and seven bamboo poles. Some supplies are provided in our tackle box.

games

Badminton, cornhole (bean-bag toss) and ping-pong. Badminton equipment and ping-pong items are provided. Our regulation takraw court with durable net can double as a badminton court since they’re the same exact dimensions, and we also have a fun badminton area, not lined yet. The covered ping-pong area is in the car park, and a second table is available if reserved in advance. The outdoor recreation and game area is available 8am until 6pm.
